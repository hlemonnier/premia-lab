Six diagnostic variants, no tuning:
- O_D: identity precision instead of full covariance; same diagonal-relative ridge as G_D.
- G_S: full covariance, but isotropic trace-scaled ridge 0.1*mean(diag(g))*I. This scale is chosen so an arbitrary constant rescaling of GLS loss also rescales its penalty; do NOT compare dimensional full precision with an unscaled0.1*I penalty.
