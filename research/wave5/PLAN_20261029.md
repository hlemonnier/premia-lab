## Frozen variants

References: B0 V3; R1 exactly as Wave4, no retuning.

Two explanatory ablations, not standalone deployment candidates:
- A_pool: replace each published R1 risk multiplier vector by its same-day cross-sectional mean. Tests whether common variance scaling, rather than learned relative stock risks, explains the gain.
- A_cross: divide R1 multipliers by their same-day mean, then clip to [0.5,2]. Tests relative risk information without average scaling. Neither ablation uses future values.

Three primary candidates, all on V3, each changing one mechanism:
- H: multiscale specific variance forecasting. Recover the same daily characteristic residual u as V3. Maintain squared-residual EW estimates with half-lives 5,21,63 and the unchanged V3 specific variance d. Features at source day s are log(clip(EWh_s/d_s,0.25,4)) for these three half-lives. Predict u[s+1]^2/d[s] with GammaRegressor(alpha=1.0,max_iter=300,tol=1e-8), log link, intercept enabled. Refit at target rows 253+126*k, every fifth matured label in the preceding 504 days, with labels >=64. Floor labels at 1e-8, no upper winsorization. Inputs float64. Clip predictions to [0.5,2], blend into d with multiplier 0.5+0.5*prediction. Baseline multiplier 1 before first refit. This targets QLIKE rather than historical Sharpe and uses only three predictors; no characteristics or model-capacity sweep.
- P: volatility-scaled characteristic premia. Maintain EW63 and EW1008 second moments of the 26 characteristic factor returns. At source s, scale each of the 26 design columns by v_s=clip(sqrt(EW63/EW1008),0.5,2); the beta column is unscaled. Use the unchanged F2 two pooled ridge learners (HL1008/252, ridge0.1, linear coefficients 50/50, squares/beta slow) on these scaled causal designs. A label at u uses design at u-1 and v[u-1]. Predict target t using design and v from t-1. Final alpha is 50% unchanged F2 plus 50% this conditional prediction. Risk, costs and market sleeve unchanged. Hypothesis differs from timing premia using aggregate market volatility; no sign choice or feature selection after results.
- E: optimize against actual traded holdings. Keep V3 forecast, virtual book, sizing, risk and fixed market sleeve. For the inner allocator, use actual held_positions minus the fixed 0.05/N sleeve instead of the separately drifted synthetic inner book. This makes its linear turnover penalty match the actual outer portfolio, before the same outer gross-cap safeguard. No changes to risk or cost horizon. Track whether the outer cap activates.

No other primary variants. A single H+P combination is permitted only if BOTH H and P individually exceed R1 by >=0.005 and improve at least two of three stages. E may be combined once with the highest-scoring of R1,H,P only if E improves B0 by >=0.005 and improves at least two stages; ties prefer the simpler existing reference. No combination if its stated gate fails. No adaptive parameter or model search after seeing results.
