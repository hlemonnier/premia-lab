## Interpretation

N learns sparse signed lead-lag relationships from residual returns, not characteristic proximity. Sign stability, fixed magnitude screens, shrinkage and an alpha cap do not make its extra trading worthwhile. Gross Sharpe1.62003 is overwhelmed by3.9663% annual fees. It loses heavily to the own-history control. Its mean IC0.01522 is below G0.01539. The graph screen is a heuristic, not a calibrated graph-wide significance test.

V adds a26x26 lagged factor-surprise map. It loses to both G and its diagonal-only control. Early factor variances use a numerical floor; no alternative warmup was tried after failure.

R clips extreme individual training responses with lagged risk, while R0 scales whole dates. R's small0.00244 full-sample gain loses in two stages and the first half; it fails the fixed bar.

M and M0 learn a shrunk blend from matured forecast errors, updating every21 days after504 labels. Both underperform the existing fixed50/50 blend. They are supervised online mixtures, not reinforcement learning. This result does not establish that every adaptive combination fails.

C adds a bounded positive-semidefinite residual-risk component. C0 adds only the same marginal variances, separating co-movement from a larger risk scale. Five modes survive the fixed screen at every fit, but the covariance addition still reduces portfolio Sharpe. Large fitted eigenvalues do not prove useful future hedges. No factor-risk component was subtracted.
