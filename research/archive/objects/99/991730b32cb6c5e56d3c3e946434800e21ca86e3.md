# Frozen N0 review — concentration survives, fee robustness does not

30 October 2026. Review branch: `research-n0-frozen-review-20261030`. Frozen main: `3d668e1877541742fcb9f9a5c665ff22ba2f7c76`. No main update or external submission.

## Decision

I would not advance unchanged N0 to release review under this investigation's predeclared rule. Its historical gain survives sleeve removal and all three fixed source-removal diagnostics, but reverses with doubled and tripled transaction costs. The higher fees are stress scenarios, not the assignment's actual fee assumption. N0 still scores better at supplied fees; this is not a finding that its future population effect is negative or that it violates the brief.

The plan preceded new outcomes at commit `d2fe4ffb106103d77888506b5a1b38c3c9dd6530`. N0 was an exploratory control from the prior ten-job batch, not an independently selected new primary. All fifteen years remain exposed development data. No model, threshold, slope screen, half-life, blend, projection, cap, sleeve or solver parameter was retuned.

## Uncertainty

The fixed paired circular-block bootstrap uses 3,000 draws, seed 20261030, lengths 21/63/126, with identical dates across the seven contrasts at each length. At 63 days, unchanged N0-minus-GLS is +0.017562 with 95% interval [-0.018633, +0.053872]. The 21/126-day intervals are [-0.023126,+0.058058] and [-0.014503,+0.049127]. All seven contrast intervals include zero at all three lengths.

The 63-day centered one-sided p-value for N0-minus-GLS is 0.160946; the seven-contrast Holm value is 0.965678. This is descriptive post-selection inference for exposed data, not a correction for every prior adaptive choice. Full-path removal intervals are not evidence for deployable superiority. No bootstrap fraction is called a probability of future success.

## Direction

I retain N0 as a research lead, not the next selected submission. The useful hypothesis is a small, fast, mostly mean-reverting correction whose costs must be justified. A future experiment should isolate signal lifetime and cost-aware trading treatment while keeping the slow GLS core fixed, rather than enlarging the failed cross-stock network or searching thresholds after this outcome.

GLS remains on main. Previously measured GC is still a separate candidate worth reviewing because its historical improvement reduced rather than increased fees. GC was not rerun, combined or promoted here; unseen future ranking remains unresolved. The predeclared N0 review gate fails on the fee scenarios, so no automatic release work follows.
