## Why this question

Wave 5's A_pool retains most of R1's historical Sharpe increment; the common specific-risk multiplier changes both allocation and scale. It is not equivalent to total volatility timing because the factor covariance is unchanged. The next tests separate learning a common specific-risk state, learning factor marginal variances, and scaling the whole risk matrix. A constant-premium hindsight ceiling is not a universal bound, but more experiments also increase selection bias.

Literature motivation (abstracts and official documentation, not numerical replications): Corsi (2009), DOI 10.1093/jjfinec/nbp001, multihorizon volatility; Engle and Sheppard (2001), NBER w8554, separating marginal variances and correlations; Cederburg et al. (2020), DOI 10.1016/j.jfineco.2020.04.015, instability of out-of-sample volatility-managed portfolio combinations; official sklearn GammaRegressor, positive log-link GLM. Our daily residual proxies are not intraday realized volatility; our factor-correlation matrix is retained, not a fitted DCC model. Published results do not establish an edge here.

C (primary, common specific risk): common target y_s = mean_i(u_s,i^2 / d_{s-1,i}). Three predictors at source s: log(clip(mean_i(EW_h(u^2)_s,i / d_s,i),0.25,4)), h=5,21,63. Set common variance multiplier c_t=0.5+0.5*clip(prediction,0.5,2), bounds[0.75,1.5], neutral1 before maturity. Only d is multiplied; factor risk stays unchanged. Both virtual and traded books use it.

F (primary, factor marginal variance): fit the same Gamma settings and date schedule to factor targets y_s,j=f_s,j^2 / max(F_{s-1,jj},1e-16). For each of26 factors, predictors are log(clip(EW_h(f_j^2)_s / max(F_s,jj,1e-16),0.25,4)), h=5,21,63. Pool factors with one common GLM (no factor-ID dummy, separate fit, or sign-dependent return feature). Clip/blend to variance multipliers v_t,j in[0.75,1.5]. Set L_new=diag(sqrt(v_t)) L_old, preserving the old factor correlations and positive semidefiniteness. Specific risk d unchanged. This is a variance-rescaling hypothesis inspired by variance/correlation separation, not full DCC.

I (explanatory intercept control): same as C, but predict the arithmetic mean of the same matured y_s labels at each scheduled refit, with no predictors. Clip/blend identically. This isolates time-varying predictors from slow causal level calibration. Not eligible for deployment selection in this plan.

T (explanatory total-risk control): use C's identical multiplier c_t on BOTH d and F, with L_new=sqrt(c_t)*L_old. Same forecast as C, different risk application. This separates total-risk scaling from the relative factor/specific-risk mix. Not eligible for selection in this plan.
