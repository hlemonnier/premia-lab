# F release decision — technically viable, not promoted

29 October 2026. Isolated branch `gpt-f-validation-20261029`.

## Decision and scope

The frozen F release audit is complete. **Technical viability: PASS within the tested scope. Main-strategy promotion: NOT APPROVED under the predeclared statistical rule.** F remains the leading research candidate, with a standalone package in `handoff_f/`; the selected V3 strategy is not replaced. This is not a finding that F is inferior to V3. It is a distinction between an executable candidate and sufficient evidence to approve its promotion.

The audit plan was committed before new runs as `341008cc3460b608b0b36889a842f78939c4ceb3`. No model parameters, return forecasts, market sleeve, trading rules, scorer or promotion thresholds were retuned. The standalone candidate is committed as `1c2e39722a81e93389bd41d5b96ee28d4dac71a4`. Its core source hashes match the executed local package. No merge into main, change to v2-factor-risk or modification of handoff_v3 was made by this audit. Nothing was submitted.

## Why main promotion is still withheld

With original fees, F's full-period market beta is **0.101233**, below 0.2. Its rolling 252-day beta spans 0.04707 to 0.19958 in this history, but the brief tests full-period beta and neither figure guarantees the unknown future. All fifteen supplied years are exposed development data.

Leaving out any one of fourteen annual-sized blocks preserves F's aggregate advantage over V3 and H: minimum leave-one-block-out gains are +0.03520 and +0.02231 respectively. The same test against T changes sign. Within individual annual-sized blocks, F beats V3 in 9/14, H in 7/14, and T in 8/14. A non-deployable diagnostic removing ten influential F-H daily differences leaves +0.02443 aggregate Sharpe gain. These are stability diagnostics, not independent holdouts.

A fixed sensitivity bootstrap uses 5,000 paired circular-block draws, seed 20261101. At 63-day blocks:

| Comparison | Gain | 95% interval |
|---|---:|---|
| F minus V3 | +0.04871 | [-0.00117, +0.10070] |
| F minus H | +0.03436 | [-0.01605, +0.08464] |
| F minus R1 | +0.03579 | [-0.01616, +0.08848] |
| F minus T | +0.00253 | [-0.04101, +0.04445] |

H and T intervals include zero at 21-, 63- and 126-day blocks. Against V3 only the 126-day interval has a positive lower bound; choosing only that interval would be misleading. The original Wave6 promotion authority remains unchanged: positive lower bounds versus H at all three lengths and Holm-adjusted p<0.05, plus the other gates. Its 63-day adjusted p is about 0.179. It fails. The new random seed is a numerical sensitivity check, not new evidence or a replacement threshold.

There is no newly untouched holdout and no correction accounting for every adaptive search across this project. Passing software checks cannot certify that the historical improvement will persist. F versus simpler T is particularly unresolved. **Keep F as the technically ready leading candidate; do not silently promote it by weakening the gate.**
