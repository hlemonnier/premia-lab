## Block 1 — new information (diagnostic gate, as in ML_PLAN)

Candidate signals not used by F2. All are causal: the signal at decision t uses rows ≤ t−1.

1. **Cost** as a static characteristic (illiquidity premium; Amihud 2002). Costs are constant
   per stock, so this tests whether expensive stocks earn more.
2. **Characteristic changes** ΔX over 1 day and over 21 days (13 each), combined by a causal
   expanding ridge (ridge 0.1, same pooled design as F2, refit daily via sufficient
   statistics). One combined signal per horizon.
3. **Daily lead-lag of factor returns.** Each of the 27 F2 factor returns at t−1, times its
   causal expanding lag-1 autocorrelation coefficient, mapped to stocks through today's
   design. This tests whether daily premia have short-run autocorrelation.
4. **Market lag-1.** The causal expanding regression of each stock's residual on the previous
   day's market return (beta-conditional reaction, Hou 2007 lead-lag).

- **Metric:** daily IC with the standardized market-residual return, residualized on F2;
  incremental IC Sharpe over OOS days 1008..3778, with halves.
- **Gate:** incremental IC Sharpe ≥ 1.0 and positive in both halves. A passing signal enters
  F2 as one extra linear column, then gets a full-scorer test under the Block 2 rule (gain ≥
  0.005, beta < 0.15).

## Block 2 — cost and risk construction (full-sample tuning, fully reported)

Grid, applied to both books: cost_horizon ∈ {10, 20, 40, 80} × factor_risk_scale ∈ {0.2, 0.3,
0.5}. That is 12 runs, including the baseline (20, 0.3). Everything else is unchanged.

**Selection.** Take the best cell only if:
- (a) net Sharpe ≥ V3 + 0.01;
- (b) every grid neighbor (adjacent cost horizon, same scale; adjacent scale, same horizon)
  is ≥ V3, a flatness requirement against picking a spike;
- (c) beta < 0.15;
- (d) all solves are certified.

Otherwise V3 stays. Every cell is reported.

## Block 3 — robustness of the future score (no selection)

For V3:
- **Future beta.** The distribution of realized beta over 20-year (5,040-day) future paths by
  stationary block bootstrap (mean block 63, 2,000 paths, seed 20261029): its 95th percentile,
  and P(beta > 0.2).
- **Same for the sleeve-free E2** (sleeve 0), for comparison.
- **Cost stress.** Net Sharpe with the scorer's costs × 1.5 and × 2. Positions are
  re-optimized, because the allocator sees the higher costs.
