## Hypothesis

The characteristic signals are slow, but the second return estimator learns one-day outcomes while the allocator amortizes costs over 20 days. A forward 20-day average residual-return target may reduce transitory noise and better align estimation with holding economics. This is a fixed-horizon target experiment, NOT an optimal dynamic-control solution. Overlapping labels do not create independent observations. The horizon 20 is inherited from the current cost_horizon, not selected by score.
