**Findings:**
- F's gain survives removal of the sleeve: +0.046 on the stock book (1.3856 vs 1.3396). The
  market share barely changes. Unlike the wave-4 grid, this is not relabeled market exposure.
- Freezing the multipliers at their average level removes the gain entirely. The improvement
  comes from the *time variation* of the forecast factor and total risk: factor-level
  volatility timing (cf. DeMiguel et al. 2024).
- F and the simpler common-scaling control T are economically indistinguishable (+0.046 vs
  +0.041 on the stock book), as GPT reported.
- **Causality, own check on `handoff_f`:**
  - with inputs replaced from row 1500 or 3000, forecast rows ≤ cut are identical;
  - forecasts from truncated histories are identical;
  - later rows differ, so the test has power.

  The code read-through found features at s using rows ≤ s, labels before the target day, and
  predictions from features at t−1.
