## Interpretation

- **What GLS is.** A second estimate of the same characteristic premia. Each training day and
  stock is weighted by its inverse noise covariance; because the factor part of that noise lies
  in the regressors' span, this mainly down-weights days when factors are volatile. Averaged
  50/50 (pre-registered, not tuned) with F2's estimate, it raises the forecast's mean daily IC
  from 0.0149 to 0.0154. The book shrinks a little and gets steadier.
- **Fragility, from GPT's own attribution.** The benefit of the weighting depends on penalty
  geometry. With an isotropic ridge it nearly disappears (+0.007), and turns negative without
  the sleeve (−0.011). Ridge strength, by contrast, is a plateau (this review).
- **Selection.** GLS is the better of two primaries in its batch (CORR scored 1.5062), at the
  end of a long chain of small, individually non-significant gains on fully exposed data.
  Expect shrinkage.

## Verdict

- **Legitimacy:** pass. Causal, reproducible, standalone, no market-share confound.
- **Replace F with GLS:** recommended on expected value. The gain is consistent across stages,
  halves, fee levels, sleeve and ridge strength. The worst plausible downside is small: the
  pure-GLS variant is about F's level. But the note must describe it as a forecast combination
  whose added value is not statistically established. The decision is the user's.
