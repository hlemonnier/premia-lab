## Variants (exactly two standalone, one conditional joint)

The forecast (F2), the E2 inner book, the virtual book and the VTvol controller are unchanged in
every variant. All variants are scored with the original scorer on the same data.

### M2: volatility-managed market sleeve (Moreira & Muir 2017, inverse-volatility form)

- **Hypothesis.** Market volatility clusters (trailing-63-day vol correlates 0.54 with the next
  21 days; the 63-day vol ranges 5% to 27%). If the market's expected return does not scale
  with its volatility, scaling the sleeve inversely to recent market volatility raises the
  sleeve's Sharpe and steadies total-book volatility.
- **Rule.** sleeve_t = 0.05 · clip(sqrt(max(qm1008, 1e-12) / max(qm63, 1e-12)), 0.5, 2).
  - qm_H are exponentially weighted second moments, half-life H, of the published
    equal-weight market return (mean of each published return row).
  - The moments start from the first published row and are updated once per new row.
  - sleeve = 0.05 until 63 market observations.
  - Session reset in `forecast()`.
  - The form copies the existing VTvol controller; nothing is tuned.
- **Known confound.** The average multiplier need not be 1, so M2 changes both the timing and
  the average size of the sleeve. I report the average sleeve and realized beta. A causal
  level normalization was not chosen, because it adds a second free choice.
- **Known risks.** Cederburg et al. (2020) find vol management often fails out of sample, and
  Barroso & Detzel (2021) find trading costs eat into it. Costs here are scorer-actual.

### H1: hybrid risk model with latent residual factors (Barra/Axioma practice; Fan, Liao & Mincheva 2013 POET)

- **Hypothesis.** After the market and the 26 characteristic factors, residuals keep real
  common structure: 91 eigenvalues above the Marchenko–Pastur edge, and residual correlation
  rises with characteristic similarity (Mantel 0.51, `structure.json`). Penalizing it softly
  in the allocator lowers realized book volatility for a given forecast. Hard neutralization
  of the forecast to residual PCs was mixed in the C09 era (k = 10 hurt; k = 20 helped
  development but hurt audit). This variant penalizes rather than removes.
- **Rule** (both the virtual and inner books, through their own risk states):
  - Keep the last 1,008 rows of each state's daily characteristic residual u.
  - Every 21 absorbed rows, refresh K = 10 loadings P by block power iteration on the window
    (30 iterations at first, 3 warm-started iterations afterwards). λ_k is the window's
    variance of P_k'u.
  - Risk = diag(d_adj) + 0.3 D F D' + P diag(λ) P'.
  - d_adj = max(d − Σ_k λ_k P_ik², 0.5 d), with d the existing shrunk specific variance. The
    statistical block uses scale 1 because it is carved out of specific variance, which the
    model already carries at scale 1.
  - The beta hedge, caps, gross cap and certificates are unchanged.
  - No latent factors until 252 residual rows exist.

### J: M2 + H1, run only if both pass

## Metric and acceptance rule (fixed now)

- **Primary metric.** Full-sample net Sharpe from the original scorer (rows 253..3778, ddof 0).
- **A standalone variant passes if all of these hold:**
  - net Sharpe − 1.4447333 ≥ 0.005;
  - realized net beta < 0.15, leaving margin under 0.2 for future beta uncertainty;
  - the net Sharpe difference vs V3 is positive in at least 2 of the 3 historical stages
    (rows 253–2015, 2016–3023, 3024–3778);
  - every solve is certified;
  - local mean rebalance < 50 ms.
- **Reported, not used for selection:** paired moving-block bootstrap of the net Sharpe
  difference (2,000 draws, blocks of 63, seed 20261029); annual fees; average gross; average
  sleeve.
- **Selection.** If both pass, run J. Select J if it beats both passing standalones;
  otherwise select the better standalone. If neither passes, V3 stays.
- **Causality and runtime for a selected candidate:** the altered-future and truncation
  identity at cuts 600 / 2000 / 3500 on all decisions before each cut, and a century runtime
  check.

## Lever not tested, on purpose

Raising the fixed sleeve toward the beta limit would raise historical Sharpe almost
mechanically: by the sensitivity arithmetic, about 1.55 at total beta 0.2. That is a bet on
this sample's 14.5%/yr market premium, would put future beta right at the limit, and runs into
the brief's "mostly long the market" warning. It is not a research result, so it is not run here.
