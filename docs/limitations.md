# Limitations

**The recorded research is development evidence, not a live track record.** All supplied years were examined during research. No untouched evaluation sample is distributed or available to the public demo.

The common-premium model may react too slowly or fit unstable premia. Factor-volatility and covariance estimates can fail under changed dynamics. N0's additional effects are sparse, mostly mean reverting, and change across overlapping estimation windows. Their historical increment is small and statistically uncertain.

The explicit market position can lose. Estimated-beta hedging is noisy, and historical beta does not guarantee future compliance. Adaptive sizing and tested limits cannot rule out ruin under arbitrary unseen returns.

Linear transaction costs are the benchmark convention, not a complete real-market execution model. This is not an execution venue, order-management system, capacity study or live portfolio service.

Some historic checks retain tiny solver-precision exceptions. Existing reports distinguish failed strict equalities, diagnostic refinements and actual scored implementations. The archive does not convert exceptions into passed tests or prior measurements into newly executed checks.

The private benchmark is not included. The public demo checks usability on an independently generated fixture; it does not reproduce the hidden generator, the historical Sharpe, or a new out-of-sample evaluation.
