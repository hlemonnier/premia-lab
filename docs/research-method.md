# Research method

I separate hypothesis formation, implementation checks, development comparison and release selection. These stages answer different questions.

## Experimental contracts

A research plan states the mechanism, feature/label timing, candidate and matched control, fixed settings, development decision rule and permitted follow-through before a new scored comparison. A control is designed to explain a result, not quietly become a new predeclared winner after the fact.

All failures and zero-effect results remain part of the record. When a control becomes a follow-up candidate, that selection is exploratory and is described that way. N0 is such a case.

## Information and accounting

Features, labels, estimated betas and covariance weights must obey their publication times. A replay pays for its actual trades after position drift. An independent cashflow reconstruction checks accounting. Future mutations, prefixes, resets and stock-order checks probe different implementation failures.

The supplied one-way costs are charged once per executed trade. Higher-fee runs change the hypothetical rate in separate replays; they are sensitivities, not extra deductions from the baseline or an additional selection objective.

## Comparison and uncertainty

The historical reports use the same exposed history and scorer for paired comparisons. Full-history, stage, half, fee and sleeve comparisons provide diagnostics, not separate independent datasets. Date-block resampling addresses some dependence; it does not undo the entire adaptive search. Multiple-comparison corrections are reported with their actual scope, not presented as project-wide protection.

A lower prediction loss need not produce a better portfolio, and a higher development Sharpe is not a reliable expected gain. Model selection remains a judgment under uncertainty.

## Release discipline

The maintained runtime is one fixed policy. Research snapshots are preserved separately, including rejected models. Public CI checks the current software and archive integrity, not historical economic superiority. A separate AI review is not a human review; neither supplies a new market sample.

No new model, parameter search or statistical claim is introduced by the repository's public-project packaging.
